More than two decades dedicated to dentistry.

Dr. Hiba Alcharbaji is an experienced dentist practising in Dubai, with more than 20 years of clinical experience across general, restorative, cosmetic and implant dentistry. Her particular areas of expertise are dental implantology and cosmetic dentistry.

After her Bachelor of Dentistry, she continued with postgraduate education in Clinical Implantology and extensive continuing professional education covering implants, veneers, smile design, dental laser applications and peri-implant care. She has practised across several dental and medical centres in the UAE and has held leadership roles including Head of Dental Department and Medical Manager.

  • Bachelor of Dentistry
  • P.G. Diploma in Clinical Implantology
  • DHA Licensed
  • MOH Licensed
  • Member of the American Dental Association
  • Former Head of Dental Department
  • Experience as Medical Manager
  • Skilled in patient-centred care and personalised treatment
